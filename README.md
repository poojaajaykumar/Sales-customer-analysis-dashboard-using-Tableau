Customer Analysis & Sales Performance Dashboard
🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior and sales performance using interactive visual analytics. The dashboard provides insights into revenue trends, regional performance, customer demographics, discount effectiveness, and product category contribution to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Business Objectives

Identify monthly revenue trends

Understand regional revenue distribution

Analyze customer demographics (age & gender)

Measure discount impact on quantity ordered

Evaluate category-wise sales performance

📌 Key Insights

Revenue peaked at $2.5M in a high-performing month, indicating strong seasonality effects 

Dashboard 1

The South region contributed ~37% of total revenue, making it the top-performing region

Customers aged 40–50 generated the highest sales (~$2.0M)

Higher discount percentages showed a positive correlation with order quantity

Mobiles & Tablets emerged as the highest revenue-generating category across genders

📈 Dashboard Components

Month-wise Revenue Trend

Region-wise Revenue Share (%)

Revenue by State (Geographic Map)

Age-wise Sales Analysis

Discount vs Quantity Correlation

Gender-wise Category Sales Comparison
